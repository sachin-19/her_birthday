    A Letter

    There is a particular kind of luck in knowing someone like you, and I don’t think I say it often enough — so here it is, written down, where it can’t be argued with.

    You have this way of making ordinary days feel like they were planned. A Tuesday turns into a story. A bad week turns into something we laugh about two months later. I have stopped trying to work out how you do it and started just being grateful that you do.

    This year asked a lot of you, and you met it anyway — mostly without telling anyone how hard it was. I hope you understand how rare that is. I hope the year ahead is gentler with you. And if it isn’t, I hope you never once have to wonder who is standing next to you.

    Happy birthday to the prettiest girl I know.

    — always
